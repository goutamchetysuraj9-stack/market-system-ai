About LMO-AI Dashboard

Welcome to the Assam & Udalguri Public Procurement & Tender Intelligence Dashboard (managed under the LMO-AI framework). Our platform serves as a free, transparent, and public directory designed specifically to help local Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) and local contractors in the Udalguri district and the wider Bodoland Territorial Region (BTR) find live public infrastructure procurement notices.

Our Core Mission

Navigating massive government procurement engines can often be complex for smaller rural builders. LMO-AI bridges this information gap by parsing data lines from public domain feeds and securely categorizing them into scannable budget tiers—ranging from micro-projects to large state infrastructure layouts. This transparency empowers local operations to quickly find official documentation pipelines without running into interface overheads.

Data Credibility & Transparency

We believe in maintaining absolute platform integrity. Every contract trace displayed on our system maintains its direct reference coordinates back to official Indian government networks, including the Assam Procurement Portal and GeM networks. This platform does not generate custom profiles, process transactional bids, or capture subscription credentials, maintaining a pure informational public service index.
