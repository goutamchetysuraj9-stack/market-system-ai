About LMO-AI Dashboard

Welcome to the Assam & Udalguri Public Procurement & Tender Intelligence Dashboard. Our platform serves as a free, transparent public directory designed specifically to help local MSMEs and contractors in the Udalguri district and Bodoland Territorial Region (BTR) track public infrastructure procurement notices securely.

Our Core Mission

We bridge the information gap by parsing public domain feeds and categorizing them into budget tiers. This empowers local entities to quickly locate official documentation pipelines without running into operational interface complexities.
