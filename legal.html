1. Terms of Service

Welcome to MarketSystem AI. By accessing this dashboard, local contractors agree to comply with automated tender verification systems and regional public procurement acts.

  • Contractors must independently verify technical specifications before official document submission.
  • Users are responsible for ensuring active GST and standard local registration licenses are maintained.

2. Privacy & Data Integrity

We respect data sovereignty. Our automated algorithms securely parse public tender sources without caching non-public proprietary firm data.

Note: Custom tender probability models and historical win-loss inputs are completely encrypted and stored locally in your deployment session repository.

3. Bidding Regulations & Fair Play

To avoid non-compliance or blacklisting on government/municipal boards, the dashboard restricts algorithms from generating anti-competitive collaborative structures.

Please verify local authority bylaws (e.g., Central Vigilance Commission rules or local municipal acts) regarding automated estimation tools prior to applying final seals.
