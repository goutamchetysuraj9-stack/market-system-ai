About Local Market Opportunity AI

Automated Public Procurement Tracker for Assam & Udalguri

LOCAL MARKET OPPORTUNITY AI (LMO-AI) ek modern, AI-powered intelligence platform hai jise local contractors aur small businesses ke liye design kiya gaya hai.

Hamara system automatic tarike se Assam Govt aur Bodoland Territorial Region (BTR) ke alag-alag departments se active tenders aur contracts ka data collect karta hai. Iska main maqsad Udalguri aur aas-paas ke ilakon ke contractors tak sahi aur sateek jankari bina kisi login jhanjhat ke pahunchana hai.

Yeh poora portal fully automated hai aur iska data matrix lagatar sync hota rehta hai taaki aapko har chote aur bade public procurement projects ki live update milti rahe.
